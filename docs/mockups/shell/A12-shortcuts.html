Pointing at an atom

C H N O S P F IChange it to that element
LChange it to chlorine (Cl)
BChange it to bromine (Br)
+ −Add a positive or negative charge
1 2 3Add a single, double or triple bond
EnterType a label, such as OH or CH3

Pointing at a bond

1 2 3Make it single, double or triple
WMake it a wedge
HMake it a hash
