Status-bar notices

The hint slot of the editor status bar, full window width. Info: --text, no icon. Error: warning icon, --warning. Each lasts 8 s, until the next canvas action, or Esc.

infoCopy a selection (button, Ctrl+C, Edit or context menu)

infoCopy with nothing selected

Copied the whole drawing. Paste into PowerPoint or Word with Ctrl+V.

infoCut

Cut. Paste into PowerPoint or Word with Ctrl+V.

infoCtrl+C on an empty drawing (the button is disabled)

Nothing to copy yet. Draw something first.

infoCopy of a very large drawing (picture made smaller, R3)
