For chemistry teachers

Chemistry drawings for your lessons, in seconds.

Chalkbond is a small Windows app for drawing structures and reaction schemes. Type a compound’s name, tidy the reaction, and paste it straight into PowerPoint or Word, sharp on every projector.

What it does

Made for the way chemistry is taught.

Built around grade 11 and 12 organic chemistry: the names you use, the reactions you write on the board, and the mechanisms you explain.

01

Type a name, get the structure.

Start typing ethanol, benzene or acetic acid and pick it from the list, by IUPAC or common name. Or write a whole reaction, ethanol + acetic acid -> ethyl acetate + water, and Chalkbond draws every part of it.

More than 400 compounds are built in. SMILES works too.

02

Reactions that line themselves up.

Add arrows, plus signs and equilibrium arrows, then type the conditions above or below the arrow. H₂SO₄ gets its subscripts by itself. Press Tidy reaction and everything sits on one line with even spacing.

03

Skeletal or structural, in one click.

Switch the whole drawing to structural formula and Chalkbond writes every CH₃ and CH₂ for you, with longer bonds so they are easy to read. One more click takes it back to skeletal.

Show structural

04

Mechanisms, the way you teach them.

Curly arrows for electron pairs and fishhooks for single electrons. They stay attached to their atoms and bonds when you move things around. Add lone pairs, charges, radicals and δ+ / δ−.

Into your slides

Copy, then paste into PowerPoint or Word.

Press Copy and paste. The drawing arrives as a crisp picture with a transparent background that stays sharp when you resize it. Paste it back into Chalkbond later and you can edit it again.

Ctrl + C Ctrl + V

  • Slides or Handout size: choose how big it lands in Office.
  • Export PNG, SVG or PDF. Exported files open in Chalkbond again, ready to edit.

And the small things

Quiet details that save you time.

  • Works offline

    No internet, no account, nothing sent anywhere. It works the same in a classroom without Wi‑Fi.

  • Saves itself

    A drawing with a file name saves as you work. Close the app any time: every tab comes back next time, undo included.

  • Tabs

    Keep a lesson’s drawings open side by side and switch with Ctrl+Tab, just like browser tabs.

  • Chemistry checks

    Impossible valences are flagged, and the formula and molar mass of your selection show as you work.

  • Number keys

    Every tool sits on a key from 1 to 0, and the shortcuts list shows every key in one place.

  • Rings and labels

    Benzene, cyclohexane or naphthalene in one click, a Kekulé switch, compound numbers, captions, brackets and colour.

Install

Ready in about a minute.

  1. Download

    Get Chalkbond-1.0.0-setup.exe. It works without internet from then on.

  2. Run it

    The installer is not signed yet, so Windows may say Windows protected your PC. Click More info, then Run anyway.

  3. Start drawing

    Chalkbond installs for your user only, with no administrator rights needed, and opens by itself. It is in the Start menu and on your desktop.
